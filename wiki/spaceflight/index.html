Terminal Burn / Field guide

Spaceflight

Energy, orbital motion, and relativity shape humanity’s reach.

Travel within a system

The initial development roadmap includes Hohmann-transfer fleet management: planning journeys between orbits is part of managing an expanding civilization.

Torchships

In the fiction primer, antimatter-powered torchships carry humanity between stars, reaching a tenth of the speed of light. Their fuel represents both an enormous investment of energy and a devastating potential weapon.

Interstellar distances

Humanity’s expansion remains small against the scale of the Milky Way. The roadmap places neighboring star systems and interstellar trade in later expansions.

Terminal Burn is in development. These introductory articles summarize the public website’s design and setting; planned features may change.
