Terminal Burn / Field guide

Universe

Humanity has spread into the void, but the galaxy remains vast and unfamiliar.

The age of expansion

The fiction primer places the first human torchship ten thousand years in the past. Expansion has become a wave, yet occupies only a small part of a galaxy a hundred thousand light-years across.

Coexistence and brinkmanship

A new system has resources enough for billions. There is room for coexistence, but the energy stored aboard interstellar ships makes war potentially cataclysmic.

Work beyond a lifetime

Building a home among the stars is a generational project. People contribute to transformations whose final results they will never see.
