Terminal Burn / Field guide

Colonies & industry

A settlement is the beginning of a supply chain that can eventually span an entire star system.

Build a foothold

Colony growth and industrial supply chains are central to the planned game. A young colony must establish the infrastructure that makes further expansion possible.

Transform worlds

Terraforming is a long-term undertaking. The setting emphasizes projects that take generations to turn barren worlds into gardens.

Alien environments

Liquid water alone does not make a planet a new Earth. The setting includes anoxic worlds and biochemistries incompatible with human life, alongside much more extreme planetary environments.

Terminal Burn is in development. These introductory articles summarize the public website’s design and setting; planned features may change.
