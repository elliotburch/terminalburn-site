Terminal Burn / Field guide

Game overview

Terminal Burn is a hard science-fiction colony simulator about turning an alien star system into a home.

First landfall

Arrive in an uninhabited system and establish a foothold. Colony growth and supply chains form the foundation for expansion across its worlds.

A civilization across generations

The campaign grows from a first settlement into an interplanetary civilization. The home page describes a progression through industrial growth, mature fleets, and eventual exploration of neighboring systems.

Development

The published roadmap includes star system generation, planetary scenes, orbital fleet management, colony growth, basic terraforming, and strategic combat. Later expansions describe deeper society systems, interstellar networks, and exotic physics.
